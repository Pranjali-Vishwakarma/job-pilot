# job-pilot

This is a tool that you can use to find Job Openings in your Dream Companies in just a few clicks, based on your Portfolio and Job Role Expectations.
Also it helps you rank your skills/projects/experiences that deserve the most to go in your resume for a selected Job Description of Company and creates a Resume out of it.
Bonus: You also get to know what skills you can work on, so that next time you apply for such a role, you learn it till then.
